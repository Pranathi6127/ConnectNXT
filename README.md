# ConnectNXT

**AI-Powered Last-Mile Connectivity for India**

Millions of Indians live within reach of metro and bus corridors — yet can't access them reliably. ConnectNXT bridges that gap with intelligent, affordable, multi-modal micro-transit.

## The Problem

Last-mile transit in India is broken:

- **60%** of Indian commuters walk over 1 km to reach the nearest public transit stop
- **45 min** average wait time for feeder buses in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities
- **₹8,200 Cr** in productivity lost annually due to last-mile transit failures

**Root cause:** rigid, infrequent, poorly routed feeder services that don't adapt to real demand.

## The Solution

ConnectNXT is built on three integrated layers:

| Layer | Description |
|---|---|
| **AI Layer** — Dynamic Routing Engine | ML model analyzes real-time demand signals (GPS pings, app bookings, transit card taps) to re-route feeder vehicles every 15 minutes |
| **Physical Layer** — Multi-Modal Micro-Hub | Shared e-bikes, auto-rickshaws, and minibuses converge at smart hubs co-located with metro stations — one QR, one fare |
| **Access Layer** — Offline-First Passenger App | Works on 2G/SMS with regional language support; integrates UPI, NCMC cards, and WhatsApp for zero-smartphone access |

## How It Works

1. **Demand Sensing** — Predicts 5-minute zone demand from app usage, card taps, and GPS
2. **Route Optimization** — RL model generates feeder routes to minimize wait times
3. **Fleet Dispatch** — Dynamic manifests; e-bikes auto-unlock at hubs
4. **Passenger Guidance** — Push alerts via app, SMS, and WhatsApp with ETAs

This closed-loop system continuously senses, optimizes, dispatches, and guides — turning chaotic last-mile transit into a predictable, seamless experience.

## Projected Impact

*Based on a 90-day simulation, Bengaluru East Corridor pilot*

- **38%** reduction in average commute time
- **2.4×** increase in transit ridership
- **55%** drop in last-mile carbon emissions
- **₹400 Cr** estimated annual savings for the city
